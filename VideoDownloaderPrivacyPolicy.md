**1. Information Collection by Third-Party Advertising Partners (Google AdMob):**

Our App uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service, to display advertisements to you. As a result, AdMob may collect and process certain information from your device to serve relevant ads, personalize your ad experience, and measure ad performance. The types of data that AdMob may collect include, but are not limited to:

* **Advertising ID:** A unique, user-resettable identification number for advertising purposes, provided by your device's operating system.
* **Device Information:** Details about your device, such as the device model, operating system version, language settings, screen resolution, and other technical specifications.
* **Network Information:** Information about your network connection, including your IP address, network type (e.g., Wi-Fi, cellular), and mobile operator.
* **Usage Data:** Information about how you interact with the ads displayed within our App, such as clicks, views, and engagement.
* **Location Data (Inferred):** While our App does not directly request or collect your precise location, AdMob may infer your approximate location based on your IP address or other network signals.

The collection and use of this data by AdMob are governed by Google's Privacy Policy, which we encourage you to review: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

"Video Downloader" itself does not directly request, collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information from you. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location (directly), contacts, photos, or any other personal details.

While we do not directly collect your personal data, AdMob does, and therefore your rights regarding that data are primarily managed by Google. However, we want to inform you of the following general rights that may be applicable depending on your location:

You may have the right to:

* **Know:** Request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information AdMob has collected about you, the sources of that information, the purposes for collecting it, and the categories of third parties with whom it is shared.
* **Opt-Out of Sale:** Opt-out of the "sale" of your personal information. While we do not directly "sell" your data, the sharing of data with advertising partners for personalized ads might be considered a "sale" under the CCPA. You can typically manage your ad personalization preferences through your device settings and Google's Ad Settings.
* **Deletion:** Request the deletion of your personal information that AdMob has collected, subject to certain exceptions.
* **Non-Discrimination:** Not be discriminated against for exercising your CCPA rights.

To exercise these rights with respect to the data collected by AdMob, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy and their mechanisms for managing your privacy settings.

**For Users in the European Union (General Data Protection Regulation - GDPR):**

You may have the following rights:

* **Right to Access:** Request access to the personal data AdMob holds about you.
* **Right to Rectification:** Request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
* **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request the deletion of your personal data under certain circumstances.
* **Right to Restriction of Processing:** Request the restriction of the processing of your personal data under certain circumstances.
* **Right to Data Portability:** Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format and have it transmitted to another controller.
* **Right to Object:** Object to the processing of your personal data for certain purposes, including direct marketing.
* **Rights in relation to automated decision-making and profiling:** Not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, which produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you.

The legal basis for AdMob's processing of your personal data may include your consent (where applicable), legitimate interests (such as serving relevant ads), and compliance with legal obligations.

To exercise your rights under the GDPR with respect to the data collected by AdMob, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy and their privacy controls.
